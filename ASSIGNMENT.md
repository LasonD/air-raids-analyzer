# KSE Agentic AI School — Stage 2 assignment

Transcribed from the invitation email (Computer Science Department, KSE, 9 Oct 2026).

**Deadline: Monday, 12 October 2026, 23:59 Kyiv time (EEST).**

## Task

A mini pet project in Python on a real-world defense problem: **time series analysis of air raid alerts in Ukraine.**

No domain expertise in time series forecasting or defense analytics is expected; AI must be the primary engineering tool used to bridge that gap ("AI is not an ornament").

## Data and evaluation

- Work with the most recent data available, and evaluate the solution on the latest period.
- **No data leakage:** nothing the model is trained on or uses as input may come from after the time at which the forecast is made.

## Optional: text and media data

Encouraged, not required: go beyond the alert history and use text or media data — e.g. news, official statements, public Telegram channels. Same rule: only content published before the forecast time.

## Submission (via the Stage 2 form)

1. **The artifact** — link to a GitHub repository with the final, working Python code.
2. **The AI log** — a full text export of the conversation history with the chosen AI tool.
3. **The reflection** — plain text, ~100 words, answering:
   - What went wrong?
   - How did you adjust your approach?
   - Why is your final version better than your first attempt?

## How it is judged

The process, not just the result. The AI interaction log is the primary signal: they look for candidates who actively iterate, challenge model hallucinations, correct errors, and guide the AI toward a functional architecture rather than accepting the first output.

Top applicants go to Stage 3: a technical and motivational screening with two interviewers, including a discussion of the solution.
